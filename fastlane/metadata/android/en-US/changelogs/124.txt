- Day length: shown as hours and minutes (10h 38m).
- Heat index and wind chill: shown next to the temperature when they apply, with a thermometer icon. The hourly and 10-day forecasts have a tab for each.
- Values in two units: the second unit is now smaller and under the first one.
- Charts: a missing value leaves a gap. Sliding along a chart scrolls the cards smoothly.
