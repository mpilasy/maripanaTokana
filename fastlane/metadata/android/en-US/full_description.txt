maripànaTokana (Malagasy for "a single thermometer") is a weather app for Android. It shows every value in metric and imperial units side by side, so you never have to convert. It is also available as a web app at maripana.njarasoa.org, with nothing to install.

<b>Weather at a glance:</b>
• Main card: current condition, temperature to one decimal place, today's low and high, rain or snow when it is coming, air quality when it is not good, and tiles for the next 4 hours
• Free-scroll layout: below the main card, all other cards are always open
• Hourly and 10-day forecasts with tabs: Conditions, Precipitation, Wind, Pressure, UV and Air quality, each with an overview chart
• Minutely nowcast when rain or snow is coming
• Current Conditions and Sun & Air tiles: feels like, wind compass and gusts, pressure, humidity and dew point, UV index, air quality (US and EU AQI), visibility, sunrise and sunset
• Activities: running, drying laundry and sun protection
• Meteocons weather icons, with day and night versions

<b>Units:</b>
• Every value in metric and imperial units side by side
• Tap a value to swap which unit comes first

<b>Weather data and alerts:</b>
• Weather from Open-Meteo (default, no API key needed) or Pirate Weather (with your own API key)
• Weather alerts from 7 sources: NWS, NHC, MeteoAlarm, JMA, ECCC, BOM and GDACS. You can turn off each source
• Pull down to refresh. The app also refreshes automatically when the data is old

<b>Places:</b>
• Search by city, postal code, coordinates or Plus Code
• Save favorites and swipe between them
• Location details card with the coordinates and the Plus Code

<b>Languages and display:</b>
• 8 languages: Malagasy, Arabic, English, Spanish, French, Hindi, Nepali and Chinese. Tap the flag to change the language
• Local digits for Arabic, Hindi and Nepali
• Right-to-left layout for Arabic
• 22 font pairs to choose from
• Share any card as an image
• Help and About pages

<b>Widgets:</b>
• Two home-screen widgets: compact (4x1) and detailed (4x2)

<b>Privacy:</b>
• Your location is used only to get the weather
• No account, no ads, no tracking
• No personal data collected
• Free software (GNU GPL v3 or later)
